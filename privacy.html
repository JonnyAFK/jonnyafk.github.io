Universal Privacy Policy for All Jon Stevers Apps

Effective Date: September 18, 2026

Developer: Jon Stevers

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications developed and published by Jon Stevers (“the Developer”). All apps under this developer name are designed with privacy in mind and collect only the minimum information required for the app to function. The Developer does not sell, rent, or share user data for advertising or marketing purposes.

2. Information We Collect

Most apps developed by the Developer collect no personal data. If an app requires limited information to operate (such as usernames, notification endpoints, or basic usage data), the app will only collect what is strictly necessary for core functionality.

Examples include:

  • Account information (only if the app requires an account)
  • Notification endpoints (push tokens, webhook URLs, email-to-SMS addresses)
  • Operational data required to send or receive app-specific signals

No sensitive personal information is collected.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Any information collected is used solely for operating the app, delivering user-requested notifications, maintaining reliability and security, and providing customer support. Information is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or marketing.

4. Disclosure of Information

The Developer does not sell or share personal information with third parties. Information may be shared only with service providers required for app functionality or when required by law.

5. Data Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect any stored information. While no system is perfectly secure, industry-standard practices are followed to reduce risk.

6. User Rights

Users may delete their account (if applicable), remove notification endpoints, request deletion of stored personal information, or contact the Developer for privacy questions.

7. Children’s Privacy

Apps developed by the Developer are not intended for children under 13 and do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The “Effective Date” will be updated when changes occur.
