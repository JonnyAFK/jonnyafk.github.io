Help Launch Let’s Play Pulse on Google Play

Google Play requires new indie apps to complete a closed test with real users before going live. If you're on Android, you can help unlock the public release.

How to join the test:

  1. Tap the button below.
  2. Become a tester on Google Play.
  3. Install the app from the Play Store.
  4. Use it normally for the next couple weeks.
